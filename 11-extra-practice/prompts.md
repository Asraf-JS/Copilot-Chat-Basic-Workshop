# 11 - Extra Practice: Prompts

Prompts for the four extra exercises. Hover over a grey box and click the copy icon in its top-right corner.

---

## Exercise A: Writing Coach

**Session:** Writing Coach, or a new chat | **Grounding:** your pasted text

```
Help me improve this announcement to managers of new staff. Keep all the facts, make it friendlier and clearer, and keep it under 120 words. Tell me what you changed and why.

Draft:
Please be informed that the laptops for new hires will be available for collection. Collection will take place on the stated date at the Admin and Facilities counter, Level 15, Menara Teratai, between 10.00 am and 12.00 pm. Staff are required to bring their staff ID. Laptops will be issued only to the staff member concerned. Any enquiries may be directed to the Admin and Facilities department.
```

Follow-up:

```
Make the opening friendlier, and keep it under 120 words.
```

## Exercise B: Make an image

**Session:** new chat | **Grounding:** none

```
Create a clean, modern image for an office notice about new laptop collection day. Show a neat stack of closed laptops on a reception counter in a bright Malaysian office, with a few potted plants. Flat illustration style, blue and white colours, plenty of empty space at the top. No text, no words, no logos.
```

Follow-up:

```
Use a calmer colour scheme with blue and white, and make the laptops the clear focus.
```

## Exercise C: Prompt Coach

**Session:** Prompt Coach | **Grounding:** your pasted text

```
My colleague typed this prompt and got a poor result: "write a purchase memo". Improve it so it gives Copilot a clear goal, context, source and expectations. Assume the memo is for a head of department in a Malaysian company, about buying office equipment. Explain each improvement in one line.
```

---

## Exercise D: Research with sources

**Session:** new chat | **Grounding:** web

```
How should a company in Malaysia dispose of old office laptops responsibly? Cover data wiping, e-waste rules and options such as resale, donation or recycling. Use official or reputable sources, show them, and keep it to five points.
```

Follow-up:

```
Which of your five points come directly from an official source, and which are general good practice? Mark each one.
```
